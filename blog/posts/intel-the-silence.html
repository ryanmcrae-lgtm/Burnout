INTEL BRIEF:
The Silence in Grid 9

[ INTELLIGENCE BRIEFING // KOROB OUTPOST // CLASSIFICATION: GENERAL ]

Three recon teams entered Grid 9 over the past standard month. None returned. Comms cut at the same coordinates each time — 44.7 clicks from Outpost Sable.

We are not yet classifying this as hostile contact.

We are classifying it as unknown. Unknown is worse.

// ---- //

WHAT WE KNOW

Grid 9 was last formally surveyed in Year 11 of the colony. At that time it was rated as low-risk transitional terrain — the kind of geography that looks like nothing until you're in it, and then looks like everything. Dry river basin. Scattered infrastructure ruins from the early construction push. Wind patterns that distort acoustic signals.

All three recon teams reported standard conditions up to the 44.7 mark. No anomalies flagged. No hostile signatures on preliminary scan. Then: silence.

Not static. Not signal degradation. Silence. Like the transmission was never started.

[ INTEL NOTE ]

The absence of a distress signal is not the same as the absence of distress. It may mean the situation resolved cleanly. It may mean resolution happened faster than transmission was possible. We do not know which.

WORKING THEORIES

Command has authorised the following working theories for field operator awareness. None are confirmed. All are active:

  • Atmospheric interference: Seasonal ionic disturbances in the basin could be absorbing signal. If so, teams may be intact and unaware of their comms failure.
  • Structural hazard: Subsurface collapse in the old infrastructure zone. Three independent teams hitting the same coordinates is consistent with a single physical trap rather than three separate incidents.
  • The Quiet: Operators with extended Korob service know what this means. We will not elaborate in a general-access briefing.

WHAT THE SILENCE MEANS — FOR YOU

Grid 9 is not a place on a map. It is a condition.

You have been in Grid 9. Most operators have. It's the period where external signals stop arriving cleanly — where you lose the feedback loops that tell you how you're doing, whether what you're doing matters, whether anyone can see you. The comms just... cut. And you keep moving because what else do you do.

The mistake the lost teams made — if they made a mistake — is treating silence as safe passage. The absence of incoming threat signals is not the same as safety. It may mean you've moved beyond the range of what can warn you.

CURRENT STATUS

Grid 9 remains flagged for restricted access. Solo entry is prohibited. Buddy protocols are mandatory.

If you are currently in a period of extended silence — externally or internally — do not proceed alone. Buddy protocols exist for exactly this condition. Find your operator. Report your coordinates. Stay on the line.

// ---- //

We will update this briefing when we have more information. We do not currently have more information. In the meantime: know where Grid 9 is in your own terrain. And don't enter it without someone who knows where you are.
