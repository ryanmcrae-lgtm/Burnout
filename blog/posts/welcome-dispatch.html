INITIAL TRANSMISSION:
Welcome to Planet Burnout

[ INCOMING TRANSMISSION // KOROB OUTPOST // PRIORITY: HIGH ]

You survived the crossing. You found us. That means you already know something most people don't — that the old world's rules don't apply out here.

Planet Korob doesn't care who you were. It only cares what you do next.

// ---- //

People arrive here burned out. That's not a metaphor — though it is that too. They arrive having pushed past the limits of whatever life they were living before. The corporate ladder. The endless hustle. The grind that promised something better at the end and delivered only more grind. They arrive hollow, ash-mouthed, eyes like they've forgotten what it felt like to want something.

Then something strange happens.

Korob burns them out the rest of the way. Down to nothing. And in that nothing — something grows.

[ FIELD NOTE ]

Every operator on this planet was broken before they were useful. That's not a bug. That's the process.

The book you're about to read — or that you've already read, or that brought you here — isn't a self-help manual. There are no seven steps. No morning routines. No productivity hacks that will fix the thing that's cracked inside you.

It's a field report. From the other side of burnout.

Welcome to Planet Burnout. You're already here. Now let's figure out what you do with that.

// ---- //

This blog — what we call the Field Reports channel on the Korob Outpost Dispatch Network — publishes transmissions from the surface. Survival logs. Intel briefings. Lore archives pulled from the margins of the book. And dispatches from operators who've made it through.

Use the filter buttons on the main listings page to find what you need:

  • DISPATCH — Updates and announcements from command
  • SURVIVAL LOG — Practical field guidance for surviving burnout
  • INTEL — Intelligence briefings on the territory ahead
  • LORE ARCHIVE — The mythology and world of Planet Burnout

And while you're here — if you haven't generated your call sign yet, head back to the main terminal. Every operator on Korob has one. It's how this place knows you.
